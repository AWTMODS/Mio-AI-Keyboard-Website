Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Created by Aadith C V (AWTMODS)

🍃 Core Privacy Principle: Mio AI Keyboard was built with the fundamental belief that what you type on your phone is strictly private and belongs solely to you.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mio AI Keyboard ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Aadith C V, handles your information. When you use an Input Method Editor (IME), you trust it with every character, message, password, and search query you type. We take this responsibility with the utmost seriousness.

2. Zero Keystroke Logging Policy

Mio AI Keyboard strictly adheres to a zero-keystroke-logging design:

  • No Keylogging: We never record, transmit, monitor, or upload your keystrokes to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or telemetry trackers) embedded in the typing path.
  • Password and Sensitive Fields: When you enter password or sensitive credential fields, the keyboard automatically bypasses word prediction, history recording, and composing caches.

3. On-Device Storage & Processing

All prediction models, Trie dictionaries, learned personal words, and custom user preferences are stored strictly inside your device's private sandboxed application storage:

  • Local Trie Dictionary: Autocomplete suggestions are evaluated in real-time within local device memory (RAM) and cached using local Room/SQLite databases that never synchronize over the internet.
  • Themes and Sound Settings: Your preferred mechanical switch audio files and theme color palettes reside locally on your device.

4. Android Permissions Explained

Mio AI Keyboard requests only the minimum required Android permissions necessary to deliver its rich typing experience:

  • VIBRATE — Required to deliver tactile haptic feedback response on physical keypresses.
  • RECORD_AUDIO (Optional) — Only used if you explicitly trigger voice typing / dictation features. Audio is processed on-device or via the Android speech recognizer service and is not retained by Mio.
  • INTERNET (Optional / Opt-in) — Only utilized when the user explicitly triggers cloud-assisted AI rewriting or translation tools. The primary keyboard typing loop itself has zero network access and functions completely offline.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional) — Requested solely if you choose to set a custom photo or wallpaper as your keyboard background.

5. Optional AI Features

When utilizing AI-assisted features (such as grammar correction or tone rewriting), requests are only dispatched when you deliberately tap the AI assistant action. Even then, only the explicitly selected snippet of text is provided, and no identifying personal device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, MAC address) are ever bundled with the request.

6. Children's Privacy

Mio AI Keyboard does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13. Because our keyboard gathers zero personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.
